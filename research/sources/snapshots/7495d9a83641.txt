# AWS WAF logging destinations

This section describes the logging options that you can choose from for your
AWS WAF logs. Each section provides guidance for configuring logging including
information about any behavior that's specific to the destination type. After you've
configured the logging destination, you can provide its specifications to your protection pack (web ACL)
logging configuration to start logging to it.
