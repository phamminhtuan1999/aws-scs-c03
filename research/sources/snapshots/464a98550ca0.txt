# AWS Certification

AWS Certification helps learners build credibility and confidence by validating their cloud skills with an industry-recognized credential and organizations identify skilled professionals to lead cloud initiatives using AWS.

We offer role-based certifications to validate skills for those in Cloud Practitioner, Architect, Developer, and Operations roles. AWS Certifications also help organizations identify and validate advanced skills in specific technical areas. View our learning paths to find training to help you build skills and prepare for AWS Certification.

## AWS Certifications

### Business

Validate strategic expertise in applying cloud and AI to business outcomes. No technical experience needed. (AI Business Strategist)

### Foundational

Knowledge-based certifications that validate AWS Cloud and AI fundamental knowledge as well as core concepts. No prior experience needed. (Cloud Practitioner and AI Practitioner)

### Associate

Role-based certifications that showcase knowledge and skills on AWS and build your credibility as an AWS Cloud professional. Prior cloud and/or strong on-premises IT experience recommended. (CloudOps Engineer, Developer, Data Engineer, Solutions Architect, and Machine Learning Engineer)

### Professional

Role-based certifications that validate advanced skills and knowledge required to design secure, optimized, and modernized applications and to automate processes on AWS. 2 years of prior AWS Cloud experience recommended. (Generative AI Developer, Solutions Architect, and DevOps Engineer)

### Specialty

Validate expertise in specific AWS technologies and services. Refer to the exam guides on the exam pages for recommended experience. (Security and Advanced Networking)

###

How should I prepare for my exam?

The best preparation for certification exams is practical experience. In general, we recommend six months to two years of hands-on experience using AWS.

To prepare for your exam with confidence, use an Exam Prep Plan on AWS Skill Builder and follow the 4 steps. Plans includes exam-style questions, hands-on labs, videos reviewing each exam domain and task statement, practice assessments, flashcards, and more. Plans include domain-based training to refresh your AWS knowledge and skills. Exact materials vary by the certification exam. Some materials require an AWS Skill Builder subscription.

###

How do I register for an AWS Certification exam?

To register for an exam, sign in to aws.amazon.com/certification and select “Schedule an exam.” Next, log into your AWS account by choosing AWS Builder ID or one of the sign-in methods. Navigate to Exam Registration, followed by Schedule an exam.

###

I am a minor. Am I eligible to take an AWS Certification exam?

Candidates ages 13-17 are permitted to take AWS Certification exams with the consent of their parent or legal guardian. For more details, please see the AWS Certification FAQ page, under the “About AWS Certification” tab.

###

What if I miss my exam because of a medical emergency?

To receive support for a cancellation or a missed exam, due to a medical emergency contact Pearson.

For personal illness (with medical documentation) or for unforeseen emergency situations (documentation required), the test delivery vendors will waive the fee and allow a reschedule without fees. In emergency situations, you will be required to provide official documentation of the emergency event. This policy is noted and digitally agreed to in the test registration agreement and in the exam appointment confirmation email. Pearson VUE staff will respond to your ticket within a period of 5 business days (weekends and holidays are not included).
